TASK 3 – TUNE A CONCRETE STRENGTH MODEL
==================================================

Dataset: UCI Concrete Compressive Strength

Objective:
To predict concrete compressive strength in MPa using seven concrete ingredients and curing age.

Input Features:
1. Cement
2. Blast Furnace Slag
3. Fly Ash
4. Water
5. Superplasticizer
6. Coarse Aggregate
7. Fine Aggregate
8. Age

Target:
Concrete compressive strength (MPa).


PART (A) – DATA PREPROCESSING AND BASELINE MODEL

Duplicate Removal:

Original observations: 1030

Observations after removing duplicates: 1005

Dataset Shape After Duplicate Removal: (1005, 9)

Training Observations: 804

Testing Observations: 201

Data Splitting:
80% Training Data  
20% Testing Data  
Random State: 42

Baseline Model: Linear Regression

Cross-Validation:
5-fold shuffled cross-validation  
Random State: 42  
Evaluation Metric: RMSE

Baseline Mean CV RMSE: 10.201933364148456 MPa

Interpretation:

Duplicate records were removed before splitting the dataset. The remaining 1005 observations were divided into 804 training rows and 201 testing rows.

The Linear Regression baseline achieved a mean cross-validation RMSE of approximately 10.20 MPa.


PART (B) – HYPERPARAMETER TUNING

Pipeline Components:

1. PolynomialFeatures
2. StandardScaler
3. Ridge Regression

Hyperparameters Tested:

Polynomial Degrees: [1, 2, 3]

Ridge Alpha Values: [0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100]

Total Combinations Tested: 15

GridSearchCV was used with five-fold cross-validation to identify the best combination of hyperparameters.

Best Parameters:

PART (C) – TEST SET EVALUATION

PART (D) – MODEL COMPARISON AND EXPLANATION

Model Comparison:

Linear Regression Baseline Mean CV RMSE:
10.201933364148456 MPa

6.0938710644995675 MPa

Improvement in Mean CV RMSE:

The tuned Polynomial Ridge model performs better than the Linear Regression baseline.

The mean CV RMSE decreased from approximately 10.20 MPa to 6.09 MPa, showing an improvement of approximately 4.11 MPa.

Degree-2 Interaction Term:

One example of a degree-2 interaction term is:

Cement × Water

This term represents the combined relationship between cement and water quantities and their possible effect on concrete compressive strength.

Purpose of StandardScaler Inside the Pipeline:

StandardScaler is included inside the pipeline to prevent data leakage during cross-validation.

It ensures that the scaling parameters are calculated using only the training portion of each fold. This prevents information from the validation data from influencing the training process.
